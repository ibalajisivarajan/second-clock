Questions

Is this a medical test?

No. It's an estimate built on simple rules of thumb, meant to show which habits matter. Real biological age comes from lab measures like blood markers or DNA methylation clocks.

How is the number worked out?

Each habit falls into a range worth a set number of years, from about two and a half years younger to six years older. The total is added to your calendar age.

Does my data leave my device?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your last answers are saved only in this browser so the check remembers them next time.

Why does resting heart rate count?

A lower resting heart rate usually reflects better heart fitness. Most phones and watches can measure it, or count your pulse for a minute after sitting quietly.

Why can't the result move further?

The estimate stays within twelve years younger and eighteen years older than your calendar age, since a habit check can't justify a bigger gap.

How do I get a real measurement?

Ask your doctor about routine blood work and fitness tests. Commercial epigenetic age tests exist too, though results vary between providers.
