Frequently asked questions

Is this a medical test?

No. This tool provides an illustrative estimate for educational purposes only. Clinical measures of biological age rely on laboratory testing, such as blood biomarkers or DNA methylation analysis.

How is the estimate calculated?

Each response falls within a defined range assigned a value from −2.5 to +6 years. The sum of these values is added to your chronological age.

Is my information stored or shared?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your most recent responses are retained in this browser's local storage only.

Why is resting heart rate included?

A lower resting heart rate generally indicates better cardiovascular fitness. For most adults, 60 to 100 bpm is considered normal.

Is there a limit to the estimate?

Yes. Results are limited to 12 years below and 18 years above chronological age, as lifestyle data alone cannot support a wider range.

How can I obtain a clinical assessment?

Speak with your family physician about routine blood work and a fitness assessment. Commercial epigenetic tests are available, although results may vary between providers.
